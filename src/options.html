Manga Translate

English over Japanese manga images, made by the vision server and the translation model on your own PC (the same ones MangaVibe's Manga Translate plugin uses).

Your PC

Sites

Where to translate, and which images. The easiest way to add one: open a chapter on the site, click the extension's button and Pick on the page.
Pages: a domain like example.com (every page on it), or a pattern like https://example.com/chapter/*. Images: a CSS selector, e.g. #reader img. Min size: smaller images (icons, ads) are skipped.

Translation

Look

Keys

On a page with a site. Change them in Firefox at about:addons → the gear → Manage Extension Shortcuts, in Chrome at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

Alt+Shift+PPause: translate no more images on this tab (the chapters after it too), e.g. a manga the site already has in English. Again: go on.
Alt+Shift+THide or show the English on this tab
Alt+Shift+EEdit the translation of the image on screen

Saved translations

Each image is translated once and kept in the browser.
